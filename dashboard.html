Reading these numbers

  • These are class averages for discussion, not evidence about any student or cohort. Who chooses to take the self-check, and how many do, both move the numbers.
  • Changes of a tenth of a point or so between semesters are usually noise, especially with small groups. The spread (SD) column shows how much students differ within a group.
  • “Lowest factor” counts how many students scored a factor as their lowest of the six. Ties count for each tied factor, so the column can add up to more than 100%.
  • Semesters come from the date each response was saved: Fall runs from the start of the school year through December, Spring from January on.
  • The Part 1 statements are adapted for classroom use and have no published norms. Only the Part 2 questions have national comparison figures.
